# Code of Conduct

Jellyboxed is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part (users, contributors, maintainers) is expected to follow these simple rules.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; people have different experience levels and first languages.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- No harassment, discrimination, insults, personal attacks, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Do not publish other people's private information (including credentials, cookies, or logs containing them).
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/YousefMohsen0) or use a private GitHub security advisory if it is sensitive. Reports are handled privately and fairly.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) (version 2.1).
